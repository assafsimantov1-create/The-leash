Who it’s for

  • Any dog that pulls because it’s excited, puppy or adult, small or large
  • Owners with 5 to 15 minutes a day
  • People who prefer reward-based training with no prong, choke or shock collars
Not for every dog. If your dog growls, snaps, or lunges out of fear, please see a qualified trainer or vet behaviorist first. Every dog learns at its own pace, and some will need to repeat days.

Will my dog really stop pulling in 14 days?

Some dogs get there in 14 days, and many need longer, especially strong dogs or those that have pulled for years. The plan tells you when to move on and when to repeat a day. No guide can promise a result for every dog.

What gear do I need?

A front-clip harness, a plain 4 to 6 ft leash and small treats. The guide explains what to choose. Nothing has to be bought from us.
